Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Still deciding? Talk to us and we'll help you choose the right setup.

Can TAIMOON POS handle credit sales?

Yes. A cashier can record a sale as credit, print the customer's bill/receipt and keep the outstanding balance for later payment.

Can it work for a bar?

Yes. The system can be configured for bars and restaurants, including product sales, cashier operations and customer credit.

Can I use M-Pesa?

Yes. The POS supports M-Pesa payment recording and can be configured around the payment workflow used by your business.

Can you customize it?

Yes. We can adapt products, categories, receipts, reports and business workflows to suit your operation.
